Parking fines

Paid parking zones: Liberty Boulevard, Central Square, the Bazar-i Kalan side of the Kök-Bel road, the Regional Hospital car park and the Central Post Office. 1 som an hour, Mon-Sat 8:00-19:00, by ticket from the attendant or by Tulpar Pay.


The check is made in your browser. City Hall does not store the numbers you enter.

Fines

OffenceFine
Parking in a paid zone without a ticket10 som
Parking at a bus or marshrutka stop or on a pedestrian crossing25 som
Parking on a lawn or in a park25 som
Blocking a courtyard entrance or fire access40 som, the car may be towed

Unpaid fines double after 30 days. Appeals in writing to the Municipal Parking Department within 10 days.

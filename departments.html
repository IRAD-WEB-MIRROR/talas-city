Small grants for neighbourhood associations

Each year the City Hall gives small grants, up to 10,000 som, to residents' associations, parents' councils and neighbourhood committees for work in their own courtyard or street. The call opens in June. Applications are checked with Köpürö, the resource centre for associations at Bilim Street 23, which also helps first-time applicants fill in the form and the report. 2026 projects include:

  • Courtyard lighting, Soviet Centre, blocks 12-16 (Chyrak residents' association)
  • Playground, Semiz, Colonia Street (Semiz Parents' Council)
  • Wheelchair ramps at two polyclinics in the Northern District (Umut association)
  • Irrigation ditch cleaning, Shahr-i Kabud (Aryk Suu water users)
  • Reading corner in the Old City (Köhne Diz neighbourhood committee)
  • Benches and shade on the Station District embankment (Station District elders' council)

Questions: Public Relations and Civil Society Department, room 215.

Paying city bills

Water, heating, rubbish fees and parking fines can be paid at any Talasbank branch, at the post office, with Tulpar Pay (Services → City payments) or in Talasbank Online (Payments → Utilities → Talas). Keep the receipt: the city enterprises take up to three working days to see a payment.

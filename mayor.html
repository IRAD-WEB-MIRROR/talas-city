The Mayor of Talas

EA
Erkin Asanbaev
Mayor of Talas since 2015, appointed by the Governor

Biography

Born in Talas in 1967. Graduated in architecture from the Frunze Polytechnic Institute in 1989.

1990-2003Architect, then senior architect, Architecture and Planning Department of the Talas city administration
2003-2010Head of land and building permits
2010-2015Deputy mayor for construction
since 2015Mayor of Talas

As mayor he led the Liberty Boulevard redevelopment of 2016-2019: new paving, lamps and shopfronts from the Central Square to Communications Square. Married, three children.

The Mayor's activity

22.10.2026 The Mayor

Mayor visits boiler house No. 6 in Semiz

Mayor Erkin Asanbaev inspected the reconstructed boiler house No. 6 with Talas Jylu Tarmaktary engineers and councillor Andrés Ochoa Kaparov. Two new boilers will heat 41 blocks in Semiz and the Western Corridor this winter.

01.09.2026 The Mayor

School year begins in 118 city schools

The Mayor opened the school year at School No. 14 in Alatau Heights, repaired over the summer. Roofs were repaired at nine schools and heating replaced at four.

10.03.2026 The Mayor

Mayor meets the residents of the Northern District

Residents raised the state of the courtyard roads and the bus stop shelters. The Mayor asked the Transport and Roads Department to include six courtyards in the 2026 repair list.

29.04.2025 The Mayor

Mayor thanks the city's snow crews

At the end of the winter the Mayor presented certificates of honour to 40 drivers and road workers of Taza Shaar and the road contractor.

12.06.2024 The Mayor

Mayor opens the repaired Semiz bridge

The footbridge over the Talas River at Semiz, closed for repairs since 2023, is open again with a new deck and lighting.

Citizens' reception

First Thursday of the month, 14:00-17:00, City Hall, room 200. Register at the front desk or on 1-09 at least a week before; say what your question is about. Deputy mayors receive citizens on the second and fourth Tuesdays.

Written appeals: by post to Meriya Square 1, Talas 724000, at the front desk, or by e-mail to mayor@talas.iradgov.org. Answer within 30 days.

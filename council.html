Talas City Council

The City Council (Talas Shaar Keñeshi) approves the city budget, local rules and street names. Chairman: Mamyt Kochkorov (since 2019). Councillors are elected for four years; the last election was in November 2023.

DateSessionAgendaMinutes

Sessions are open to the public: Council hall, City Hall, second floor, 10:00. Residents may speak on an agenda item if they register at the front desk two days before.

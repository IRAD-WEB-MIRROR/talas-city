Clubs and associations

Residents' associations, clubs and societies that work in Talas and are on the City Hall's register of civil society organisations. They are run by their members, not by City Hall. Many of them take part in the small grants for neighbourhood associations.

Neighbourhood associations

  • Chyrak residents' association
    Courtyards, lighting and repairs in blocks 12-16 of the Soviet Centre.
    Meets in the courtyard office of block 14, first Monday of the month, 19:00.
  • Köhne Diz neighbourhood committee
    The lanes of the Old City: water, rubbish days, the reading corner.
    Elders meet after Friday prayers at the Köhne Diz chaikhana.
  • Station District elders' council
    Vokzal and the railway houses: the embankment, street lighting, disputes between neighbours.
    Tuesdays 10:00-12:00, the old railway club on the station square.
  • Aryk Suu water users' association
    Cleaning and sharing the irrigation ditches of Shahr-i Kabud.
    Spring meeting before the water is let in, usually the first Sunday of April.
  • Semiz Parents' Council
    Playgrounds and safe crossings near the Semiz schools.
    Through the school offices; contact via Köpürö.

Help and care

  • Umut association
    People with disabilities and their families: ramps, wheelchair loans, help with paperwork.
    Northern District, by appointment through Köpürö.
  • Köchkön families' society
    Families resettled from the villages flooded by the Toktogul reservoir; keeps the August remembrance on the reservoir shore.
    Köchkön, the mosque hall, second Saturday of the month.

Sport and hobbies

  • Tulpar Army supporters' club
    Tulpar Talas FC fans: away trips, tickets for members, the white and crimson end at home games.
    Silk Road Arena on match days. · Web page
  • Kuble Sky amateur astronomy circle
    Star nights for beginners, telescopes to try, meteor showers from the hills.
    Clear Friday nights; place announced on the circle's page. · Web page
  • Talas Quilting Circle
    Patchwork and quilting; quilts for the children's home every winter.
    Wednesdays 15:00, Köpürö, Bilim Street 23. · Web page
  • Bazar-i Kalan toguz korgool club
    Toguz korgool for all ages; city tournament during the Nooruz holidays.
    Daily from 16:00 at the chaikhana by the bazaar's north gate.

Add or correct an entry

Is your association missing, or have its details changed? Write to the Public Relations and Civil Society Department, room 215, or to info@talas.iradgov.org. The full directory of associations, with their registration details, is kept by Köpürö, the resource centre for associations.

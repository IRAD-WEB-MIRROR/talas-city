City news

04.11.2026 City

Winter in the city: the fair, the concerts and the New Year tree

The Hunarmand winter fair of the masters of Iradi crafts takes over Coppersmiths' Row on 5 December; the street is closed to cars from 08:00 to 20:00. The Philharmonic, the Opera and Ballet Theater and Ak Zal have published their December programmes, and Ak-Kuu gives its New Year concert at the Philharmonic on 27 December. The city's New Year tree on Theatre Square is lit on 26 December at 18:00.

30.10.2026 City

Leaf collection in the parks until 20 November

Leaves from courtyards can be left in bags beside the containers on collection days. Burning leaves in the city is not allowed.

28.10.2026 Transport

Winter tyres: marshrutka inspections from 16 November

The Transport and Roads Department and the traffic police will check marshrutkas at the Central Bus Station and the Semiz terminus. Vehicles without winter tyres lose their route licence until they are fitted.

27.10.2026 City Council

City Council meets on 12 November

On the agenda: the 2027 city budget (first reading), winter road maintenance and the marshrutka route licences for 2027. The session is open to the public.

22.10.2026 The Mayor

Mayor visits boiler house No. 6 in Semiz

Mayor Erkin Asanbaev inspected the reconstructed boiler house No. 6 with Talas Jylu Tarmaktary engineers and councillor Andrés Ochoa Kaparov. Two new boilers will heat 41 blocks in Semiz and the Western Corridor this winter.

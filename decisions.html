Decisions and budget

Orders of the Mayor and decisions of the Talas City Council. Full texts are kept in the reading room (City Hall, room 104, Mon-Fri 9:00-17:00) and published in the city's bulletin Talas Shaary. Orders and decisions that affect the rights of residents take effect after publication.

Orders of the Mayor

DateNo.Order
26.10.2026418-rOn the winter maintenance of city roads 2026-2027
Approves the snow-clearing plan, the list of priority roads and the duty rota of the Transport and Roads Department. Taza Shaar and the road contractor keep 38 machines on standby from 15 November.
12.10.2026402-rOn the start of the heating season
Orders Talas Jylu Tarmaktary to begin heating all connected buildings on 15 October 2026 and to report daily to the Housing and Utilities Department until all complaints are closed.
05.10.2026397-rOn small grants to neighbourhood associations, 2026
Approves 23 projects selected by the grants committee with the Köpürö resource centre, total 184,000 som. Payment in two parts; reports by 1 March 2027.
10.09.2026371-rOn preparing schools for winter
Orders the Education Office to check heating, windows and boiler rooms in all city schools by 1 October.
14.08.2026344-rOn pressure testing of the heating mains
Approves the timetable of pressure tests from 19 August to 12 September 2026 and requires notice to residents three days ahead.
22.06.2026281-rOn the extension of marshrutka route 40
Extends route 40 to the Cottonwood Estates terminus from 1 July 2026.
02.04.2026152-rOn the spring cleaning days
Declares 18 and 25 April 2026 city cleaning days. Taza Shaar provides bags and removes the rubbish the same day.
12.01.2026014-rOn the city's citizens' reception rota for 2026
The Mayor receives citizens on the first Thursday of each month; deputy mayors on the second and fourth Tuesdays.
03.09.2025355-rOn paid parking zones
Introduces paid parking on Liberty Boulevard and around Central Square from 1 October 2025, 1 som an hour, and sets up the Municipal Parking Department.

Decisions of the City Council

SessionNo.Decision
15.10.202641/1Heating season readiness
Takes note of the Talas Jylu Tarmaktary report. Asks for a report on the Semiz boiler houses at the next session.
15.10.202641/2Paid parking: extension to the Central Post Office
Extends the paid parking zone to the Central Post Office car park from 1 November 2026.
15.10.202641/3Naming of a new street in Semiz
Names the new street between the Semiz terminus and the school 'Colonia Street' (Koloniya köchösü), as proposed by the residents.
17.09.202640/1City budget: report for the first half of 2026
Approves the report. Revenue 51% of plan, spending 46%.
17.09.202640/3Talas Suu water metering programme
Approves the fitting of meters in 6,000 flats in 2026-2027, free to residents.
09.07.202639/1Summer water restrictions on the left bank
Garden watering from the mains in the Station District only from 21:00 to 07:00, 10 July to 31 August.
09.07.202639/2Bazar-i Kalan stall rents for 2027
Keeps the 2026 rents for 2027; the Finance Department to report on arrears.
21.05.202638/3Petition on the irrigation ditches of Shahr-i Kabud
Asks the Housing and Utilities Department to clean the ditches before 1 June and to restore the old water rota.
11.12.202536/1City budget for 2026
Adopts the 2026 budget (see table below).
